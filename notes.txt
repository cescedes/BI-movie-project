Pipeline results summary

The final pipeline successfully produces a clean Tableau-ready BI dataset for analyzing monthly movie popularity and audience reception. 
After filtering low-activity movies, the final warehouse contains 51,512 movie-month fact rows, 1,297 movies, 264 monthly date entries, 19 genres, and 3,653 movie-genre bridge rows.

The fact table has the grain of one movie in one month and includes the measures rating_count, avg_rating, and tag_count. 
The final model uses three main dimensions: Date, Movie, and Genre. Since movies can belong to multiple genres, Genre is modeled separately using dim_genre.csv and bridge_movie_genre.csv.

Validation results show that the dataset is consistent: there are no duplicate fact rows, no missing movie or date keys, no duplicate bridge rows, and no movies without genre assignment. 
IMDb and TMDb enrichment also worked well, with TMDb matching 1,295 out of 1,297 movies.


Analytical Questions

Q1. Which movies generate the highest audience engagement over time?

Measures: rating_count, tag_count
Dimensions: Movie, Date
Benefit: Helps identify movies with strong long-term interest versus movies that only receive short-term attention spikes.

Q2. Which genres sustain audience attention over multiple months?

Measures: rating_count, tag_count, active months
Dimensions: Genre, Date
Benefit: Shows which genres maintain durable audience interest and which genres are more short-lived.

Q3. Are the most popular movies also the most positively received?

Measures: rating_count, avg_rating
Dimensions: Movie, Genre
Benefit: Helps distinguish between movies that attract a lot of attention and movies that are actually rated highly by audiences.

Q4. How does audience reception differ across genres and release periods?

Measures: avg_rating
Dimensions: Genre, Release Year / Release Period, Date
Benefit: Supports analysis of whether certain genres or movie eras tend to receive better audience ratings.

Q5. Which movie attributes are associated with sustained popularity?

Measures: rating_count, tag_count, active months, avg_rating
Dimensions: Genre, Director, Language, Production Country, Release Year
Benefit: Helps identify movie characteristics linked to long-term engagement and positive audience reception.
